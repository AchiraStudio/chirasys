POINT OF SALE

Built for the busiest fifteen minutes of your day.

A cashier environment with zero wait states — scanning, searching, and payment all happen locally, instantly.

  • Instant search & barcode-first flow — type two letters or pull the trigger; results appear with no round-trip.
  • Category quick-tabs and grid or list views, tuned for touch and keyboard alike.
  • Split payments across cash, QRIS, bank transfer, debit/credit card, and customer credit (Piutang).
  • Cash shifts — starting cash, cash in/out, and end-of-shift drawer reconciliation.
  • Sales returns handled right at the register, stock and ledger included.

INVENTORY

Every unit, batch, and rupiah accounted for.

Multi-unit products with conversion, batches with expiry, and a complete stock ledger — connected to POS, purchasing, and accounting in real time.

  • BOX → PACK → PCS hierarchies with independent pricing per unit.
  • Batches & expiry captured at goods receiving, visible at the register.
  • Stock ledger — every movement recorded, nothing overwritten.
  • Stock Opname (physical count) with variance handling and automatic adjustments.
PURCHASING

ACCOUNTING

Books that write themselves.

Every business event becomes a balanced double-entry journal. You review; Kivo records.

  • Automatic journals from sales, receiving, adjustments, and returns.
  • Full Chart of Accounts — assets, liabilities, equity, revenue, COGS, expenses.
  • Reports — Laba Rugi (P&L), Neraca (Balance Sheet), Trial Balance.
  • Inventory valuation by Average, FIFO, or LIFO.

LAN SYNC

Your counters, in sync — no internet required.

Terminals discover each other over UDP and synchronize over HTTP on your local network. A companion to Kivo Cloud, not a replacement — ideal for multi-register stores.

  • UDP discovery — terminals find the server automatically on the network.
  • HTTP peer synchronization between the main terminal and every register.
  • Configurable permissions — decide which terminals may sync what.
  • Works alongside Kivo Cloud — the whole store syncs when any uplink returns.

SECURITY

Designed so you stay in control.

Kivo is designed around a pure Bring Your Own Key model — precise by intention, not vague by convenience.

Your keys. Your infrastructure. Your data.

  • No baked-in credentials — Kivo ships without API keys of any kind.
  • No hidden fallback secrets — nothing silently routes through our servers.
  • Local credential storage — your Supabase and OpenAI keys live on your machine.
  • Zero-trust design philosophy — nothing is assumed trusted except you.

Technical details
  • LAN terminals are discovered via UDP broadcast and synchronized over HTTP peer connections.
  • Cloud sync runs in the background with an offline queue and retry behavior — records never block a sale.
  • Credentials are configured locally; no keys ship with the binary and there are no fallback secrets.
  • Application state is managed with Zustand; styling uses the same token system this website uses.
